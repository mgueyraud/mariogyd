A padel court under floodlights, with a crowd in the stands and four tiny players playing a point on loop. Everything is built from simple boxes and drawn at a very low resolution, then scaled up with no smoothing so every pixel stays sharp. A final pass reduces the colours to a few steps with a dither pattern and darkens the shaded side of each edge, which is what makes it look like pixel art instead of a 3D render. Inspired by [Small plays](https://x.com/zzzzshawn/status/2111546123546661289) by [@zzzzshawn](https://x.com/zzzzshawn).
