A typewriter you can type on. Use your keyboard or tap the keys, and a full row feeds the paper up.

Made with the [Hairline skill](https://hairline.lucasmarkes.com/skill) by [@lucasmarkes](https://lucasmarkes.com), then tweaked to add letters, move only the carriage, and hold keys down while pressed.
