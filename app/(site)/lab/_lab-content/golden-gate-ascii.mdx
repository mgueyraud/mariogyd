The sun sets through the Golden Gate while fog pours in under the bridge. Cars trail lights across the deck, the aviation lamps blink on the towers, and a sailboat crosses the sun's reflection on the water.

Every cell is shaded in colour, then drawn as a dot sized by its brightness. The scene is modelled on [Varanasi Ghats](https://ascii.rest/varanasi-ghats/) from [ascii.rest](https://ascii.rest) by [@bas3line](https://github.com/bas3line), and it runs on that project's canvas player.
